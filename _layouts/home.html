Hi, I'm Marco Pereira, an MSc student in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Aveiro, with a background in Mechatronics Engineering from the University of Évora. I'm drawn to the point where computer vision meets robotics, and that's exactly where my thesis lives.

My work is part of the PomeAI project, developed at the Laboratory of Automation and Robotics in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at the University of Aveiro. I'm researching how to acquire multispectral imaging data, and how to build and manage a good, reliable dataset.

This blog is my weekly lab notebook: what I built, what broke, and what I learned along the way. Follow along as the thesis takes shape.
